Privacy policy

How information sent through this website is collected and used.

Information we collect

When you submit a form, we receive your name, phone number, email, the type of floor and any message you add, along with the page you submitted from. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

How it is used

Your details are used to reply to you, book an estimate and keep in touch about the job. They are never sold, rented or handed to anyone for advertising.

Service providers

Form submissions may be delivered to us by an email or automation service. Maps on this site come from Google Maps and images load from Unsplash; those services may record basic technical information such as IP addresses under their own policies.

Your rights

You can ask us to update or delete information you have sent by contacting us through our contact page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will replace what you see on this page.
