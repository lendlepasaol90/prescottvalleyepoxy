About Prescott Valley Epoxy Flooring

A local floor coating team that puts the concrete first, explains things plainly and leaves your home the way we found it.

Prescott Valley Epoxy Flooring helps homeowners and businesses transform ordinary concrete into durable, attractive surfaces built for everyday use. Serving Prescott Valley, AZ, and surrounding communities, we specialize in garage epoxy flooring, residential epoxy coatings, commercial epoxy flooring, decorative flake systems, metallic epoxy finishes, and concrete floor coatings. Whether you're upgrading a garage, refreshing a patio, or protecting a high-traffic workspace, we offer customized flooring solutions designed for Arizona's demanding conditions. Our focus is on proper surface preparation, quality materials, and professional installation. Contact Prescott Valley Epoxy Flooring today for a free estimate!

Prescott Valley Epoxy Flooring is owned by Brooks Shepard. As an epoxy flooring company in Prescott Valley, AZ, we are a local team that answers its own phone and stands behind its own floors.

Our work rests on one idea: a coating is only as good as its bond to the concrete underneath. Most floors that peel, flake or bubble were never properly attached to the slab. They were rolled over sealer, dust or damp concrete and left to fail.

So we spend our time where it matters. We test the slab, remove builder sealers and old coatings with diamond grinding, repair cracks and winter damage, and only then choose and install a system suited to how you use the space.

The kinds of projects we take on

Garages are the bulk of our work, from newer two-car garages in master-planned communities to oversized shops on acreage in Dewey-Humboldt and Chino Valley. We also coat laundry rooms, basements, casitas, home gyms and patios, and we work with local businesses on showrooms, service bays, offices, restaurants and warehouse space.

What to expect

Call or fill in the estimate form. We will arrange a time to see the floor, measure, check for sealers and moisture, and show you samples. Afterward you get a written estimate. There is no pressure to decide that day, and we are happy to answer questions as you think it over.
