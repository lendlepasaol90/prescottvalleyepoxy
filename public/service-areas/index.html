Where we work

Prescott Valley and the surrounding Quad Cities communities, with commercial projects further afield by arrangement.

Floor coating projects run on tight schedules. Coatings need the right temperature, crews need early starts in summer and projects sometimes shift when weather changes. Working close to home lets us stay flexible and reliable.

These are the areas we serve most often. If you are not on the list, call anyway. We will tell you honestly whether we can help.

Prescott Valley

Our home base. We work across the town, from established neighborhoods near the town center to newer communities like StoneRidge, Pronghorn Ranch, Viewpoint and Granville.

Dewey-Humboldt

Larger lots, detached garages, barns and workshops along Highway 69, including homes around the Prescott Country Club.

Prescott

Garages, homes and businesses just over Glassford Hill. Older Prescott homes often have slabs that need extra repair before coating.

Chino Valley

Rural properties with shops and outbuildings, plus new construction on the north side of the Quad Cities.

Mayer

Homes and outbuildings southeast of Prescott Valley, where garages often double as workshops.

Cordes Lakes

Residential garages, patios and storage off I-17.

Paulden

Ranch properties and detached garages north of Chino Valley, often with heavy equipment and trailers.

Services available throughout the area

  • Garage Floor Coatings: New-build and older garages, prepped and sealed properly
  • Residential Epoxy Flooring: Laundry rooms, casitas, bonus rooms and living areas
  • Decorative Flake Flooring: Stone-look chip floors in valley-inspired blends
  • Metallic Epoxy Flooring: Custom, flowing finishes with real depth
  • Polyaspartic Floor Coatings: Quick-curing topcoats that work in all four seasons
  • Polyurea Floor Coatings: Flexible base coats for shops, barns and cracked slabs
  • Patio & Concrete Coatings: Back patios, front entries, walks and spa pads
  • Commercial Epoxy Flooring: Retail, medical, food service and showrooms
  • Warehouse Floor Coatings: Warehouses, fleet bays, storage and light industrial
